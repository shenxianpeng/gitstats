The share of each quarter’s commits that name an AI coding assistant: as their author, as a co-author, or in the line it signs with. A lower bound: an assistant that leaves no mark is not counted. 2 projects have fewer than 30 commits in Jul–Sep 2026, too few for a share to say much: listed after the others, without a rank. Commits (bots’ included) and active authors (people only) are those of the 12 months up to each report; a comparison page counts the 12 months up to the project’s last commit, without bots. Health is the project’s pace of the last year; the assistants named most are those of all its history.

What is counted
  • The author is an assistant's own account, such as the Copilot coding agent, Devin or Cursor's agent.
  • A Co-authored-by: line names an assistant, as Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor, Codex, Aider and others add when they commit for a person. A review bot whose suggestion was applied is named the same way.
  • An Assisted-by: or Generated-by: line names an assistant.
  • The message has the line an assistant leaves in it, such as “Generated with [Claude Code]” or the link to a Devin or Codex session.

Accounts and addresses are matched, not first names: a person called Claude, Devin or Jules is not an assistant.
