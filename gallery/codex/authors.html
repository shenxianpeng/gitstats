AI-assisted commits

102 of 12,333 commits (0.8%) name an AI coding assistant: as their author, as a co-author, or in the line it signs with. In the last 12 months: 49 of 10,536 (0.5%).

These are lower bounds. An assistant that leaves no mark in the commit is not counted, and a mark does not say how much of the commit the assistant wrote.

A commit that names two assistants is counted under both.

What is counted
  • The author is an assistant's own account, such as the Copilot coding agent, Devin or Cursor's agent.
  • A Co-authored-by: line names an assistant, as Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor, Codex, Aider and others add when they commit for a person. A review bot whose suggestion was applied is named the same way.
  • An Assisted-by: or Generated-by: line names an assistant.
  • The message has the line an assistant leaves in it, such as “Generated with [Claude Code]” or the link to a Devin or Codex session.

Accounts and addresses are matched, not first names: a person called Claude, Devin or Jules is not an assistant.
