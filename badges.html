At a glance

Several numbers, or the pace of the last year, in one badge.

summaryCommits, authors and lines in one badge
[![GitStats summary](https://<your-report-url>/badges/flat/summary.svg)](https://<your-report-url>/)
activityCommits per month over the last year
[![GitStats activity](https://<your-report-url>/badges/flat/activity.svg)](https://<your-report-url>/)
healthActive, quiet or dormant, by the age of the last commit
[![GitStats health](https://<your-report-url>/badges/flat/health.svg)](https://<your-report-url>/)
